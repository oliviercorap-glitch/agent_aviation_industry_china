Reading

Peak-period equipment reliability issues at Daxing can influence future procurement decisions across the entire CAAC airport network. Any competitor equipment failures during this window would create a replacement opportunity.
Recommended action

Nanjing Airport adds Xiamen Airlines overnight capacity and expands international routes

Reading
Nanjing Lukou Airport has partnered with Xiamen Airlines to introduce based overnight aircraft capacity and expand international passenger routes. Adding based aircraft at a station increases early-morning departure peaks, which drives demand for pre-dawn ground support equipment cycles — tugs, pushback tractors, and de-icing units in winter months. International route expansion also raises widebody handling requirements.
Business impact
Based-aircraft growth at Nanjing translates directly into incremental GSE fleet requirements, particularly pushback tractors and towbarless units. If Xiamen Airlines or Nanjing Airport issues a tender for additional ground handling equipment, TLD should be positioned early. This also signals broader airline capacity commitment to secondary Chinese hubs, a positive leading indicator for GSE demand.
Recommended action
Monitor Nanjing Lukou Airport and Xiamen Airlines procurement channels for GSE tenders over the next 60 days; prepare a pre-tender technical proposal for pushback tractor supply.

Reading
