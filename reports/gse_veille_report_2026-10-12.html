Executive summary
This week's signals are dominated by competitive rather than demand-side news: Guangtai is confirmed as a full-range GSE threat directly targeting TLD's tractor, GPU and air-start lines, while CIMC Tianda and its subsidiary continue to strengthen global boarding bridge and integrated airport equipment positions, and Jiangsu Tianyi expands electric vehicle-based GSE. On the demand side, Guangzhou Baiyun set a record with over 100,000 international movements, Shandong and Inner Mongolia posted solid holiday traffic growth, and northern and plateau airports began winter deicing mobilisation, all of which support near-term equipment demand. The net effect is a market where demand is healthy but pricing and package-bundling pressure from domestic champions is intensifying, particularly in electrification and integrated tenders. TLD should respond with a rapid competitive teardown of Guangtai pricing, a focused Q4 push on deicing and Baiyun fleet renewal, and a strategic decision on localisation or partnership to qualify for green procurement.
