Executive summary
This week's signals are dominated by competitive intelligence rather than new demand events: Weihai Guangtai is confirmed as a full-line GSE threat across TLD's tractor, GPU and bridge families, while CIMC Tianda continues to bundle bridges, BHS and GSE into integrated terminal packages, and Jiangsu Tianyi expands mid-tier GSE. On the demand side, China's dual-holiday traffic surge — Wuhan at 938,000 passengers, Capital Airport above 1.66 million, Turpan passing 600,000 annual passengers — confirms robust underlying airport throughput that supports aftermarket and replacement demand. The strategic picture is a two-front squeeze: Guangtai on price and full-line breadth, CIMC Tianda on integrated packages, both leveraging Chinese cost bases for domestic and export competition. TLD must respond with differentiated TCO, electrification and service propositions rather than competing head-on on price, while locking in framework agreements with provincial airport groups before the 2027 procurement cycle.
