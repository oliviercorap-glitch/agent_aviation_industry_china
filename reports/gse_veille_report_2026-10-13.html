Shanghai Pudong T3 international main hall steel roof about to top out

Reading
The 6,000-tonne steel roof of Pudong Airport T3's international main terminal is nearing structural completion, marking a major milestone in one of Asia's largest airport expansion projects. T3 is designed to add roughly 50 million annual passenger capacity to Pudong, with commissioning targeted in the late 2020s. This is a leading indicator for a multi-year GSE procurement wave at China's largest international hub.
Business impact
A new international main terminal at Pudong implies hundreds of new GSE units over the fit-out and ramp-up phase — tractors, belt loaders, pushback tugs, deicers, catering and lavatory trucks — plus replacement of legacy diesel fleets to meet Shanghai electrification targets. TLD should position early with Shanghai Airport Authority and incumbent handlers (ground handling at PVG is dominated by a few large operators) to be specified into the initial equipment package rather than competing on spot tenders later.
Recommended action
Within two weeks, task the China sales team to map the T3 GSE procurement timeline and decision-makers, and prepare a full electric product package proposal for the T3 opening fleet.

Guangzhou Baiyun hits record 100,000+ international passenger and cargo flights

Reading
Baiyun Airport's port cleared over 100,000 inbound and outbound passenger and cargo flights year-to-date in 2026, the fastest in its history, confirming Baiyun as the dominant international gateway in South China. TLD's leasing book in the Pearl River Delta should see improved utilisation and renewal pricing power; the cargo GSE segment (loaders, transporters) is the fastest-growing sub-segment and should be prioritised in inventory planning for 2027.
Recommended action
