Ulanqab Airport hits 100,000 passengers 99 days earlier than 2025

Reading
Ulanqab Airport surpassed 100,000 annual passengers 99 days ahead of last year's pace, implying traffic growth well above 20% year-on-year at this Inner Mongolia regional airport. Fast-growing small and regional airports are the earliest indicators of equipment capacity strain, as existing GSE fleets are sized for prior traffic levels.

While individually small, the aggregation of dozens of fast-growing regional airports across Inner Mongolia, Xinjiang and western China represents a meaningful volume pool for TLD's lighter equipment lines and leasing products.
