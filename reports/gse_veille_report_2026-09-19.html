Shenzhen adds sixth off-site air cargo station, expanding domestic cargo network

Reading
Shenzhen Airport activated a new Shenzhen-Qingdao off-site air cargo station, bringing its total domestic remote cargo terminals to six. Off-site cargo stations require dedicated ground handling fleets including cargo tractors, dollies, pallet loaders and container handling equipment at each node.
